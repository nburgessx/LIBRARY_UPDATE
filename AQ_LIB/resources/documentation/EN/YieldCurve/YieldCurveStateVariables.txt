Yield Curve Interpolation State Variables


1. OIS Curve:         
Zero rates


2. Swap Curves:
(a)	FRAs/Futures section: Discount factors
(b)	Swaps section: logarithm of discount factors, i.e. r * t


3. Basis curve:
(a)	FX Forwards section: Discount factors
(b)	Basis swaps section: Either, logarithm of target curve’s discount factors, or, logarithm of spreads added on top of the base curve


4. FX Forward Constant and Cheapest-to-Deliver Curves:
Discount factors are directly implied from discount factors of the three other curves
